01 · Core concept

Four pairs of glasses, collapsed into one

Most people move between prescription glasses, reading glasses, computer glasses, and sunglasses over a single day — swapping, misplacing, or simply squinting through the wrong pair. EYE ON YOU is built around a single premise: the lens itself should change, not the glasses on your face.

Prescription, on demand

Electronically adjustable optics reproduce your verified prescription — near-sighted, far-sighted, or reading — set once by your eye-care professional.

Reads the moment

Ambient-light and motion sensors, combined with your phone's context, infer whether you're reading, on a screen, driving, or outdoors — and switch accordingly.

Never out of your control

A physical button, discreet touch control, and the EYE ON YOU app always sit above the automation — override any mode in a tap.

02 · Adaptive vision correction

Electronically adjustable optics, grounded in real ophthalmic practice

The lens uses a stacked liquid-crystal optical layer whose refractive power can be shifted electronically across a small, clinically meaningful range — the same principle used in existing electroactive-lens research, applied to a consumer frame.

Prescription profile

Sphere, cylinder, axis, and add power are entered from your eye-care professional's prescription — never inferred from a photo or camera scan.

Astigmatism support

Where the cylinder/axis values fall within the electroactive layer's correction range, toric correction is applied; higher astigmatism defers to a professionally ground base lens beneath the adaptive layer.

Distance switching

Reading, computer (arm's length), and distance (driving, outdoor) powers are stored as three anchor points; the system interpolates and blends between them.

Two ways to set it

Auto mode uses onboard sensors plus phone context to pick a distance band; Professional mode plays back exact settings an optometrist configured and locked.

Multiple wearers

The app stores separate vision profiles per person; the frame recognizes who's wearing it via paired-phone proximity and a quick fit calibration, not biometric scanning.

Fallback base lens

Every lens ships with a passive, professionally ground base correction, so vision correction never depends entirely on battery or electronics.

NOTE A phone or onboard camera cannot diagnose a prescription. Vision correction values must be entered from a valid refraction performed by a licensed optometrist or ophthalmologist, and verified in-app before the adaptive layer activates full correction.
03 · Eye Comfort Mode

Reducing the contributors to visual strain

Comfort Mode adjusts brightness response, blue-light filtering, and glare handling based on what your eyes are pointed at — not a medical treatment, but a continuous, personalized adjustment layer.

Adaptive brightness

Tint and transmission respond to ambient lux in real time, easing the jump between dim and bright environments.

Blue-light management

Evening hours shift the lens coating's blue-light transmission down, paired with your phone's own night-shift schedule.

Anti-glare mode

A polarizing layer engages selectively against reflected glare — screens, wet roads, water — without darkening the whole scene.

Reading & computer optimization

Near-distance power pairs automatically with a warmer, lower-contrast tint proven to ease sustained close work.

Reduced visual stimulation

A low-stimulation setting flattens tint transitions and disables notification-style LED cues for sensory-sensitive moments.

Comfort Profile

The system learns the brightness, tint, and blue-light levels you manually adjust toward, and quietly biases future automatic settings to match.

NOTE Eye Comfort Mode is designed to reduce potential contributors to eye strain and visual discomfort. It does not diagnose, treat, or cure headaches or any medical condition. Persistent eye strain or headaches should be evaluated by a qualified eye-care professional.

05 · Lens technology

What's actually inside the lens

Seven functional layers, laminated to roughly 2.1mm total thickness — thinner than most progressive lenses on the market today.

01
Outer protective layer

Scratch- and impact-resistant hard coat, oleophobic finish.

02
Adaptive optical layer

Electroactive liquid-crystal element that shifts refractive power for near, intermediate, and distance correction.

03
Tint / photochromic-electronic layer

Electrochromic film driven by the ambient-light sensor, blending with a passive photochromic base for fail-safe operation.

04
Base optical layer

Professionally ground passive correction — the permanent fallback prescription.

05
Sensor layer

Edge-mounted ambient-light and proximity sensors laminated at the lens periphery, outside the optical zone.

06
Electronics & interconnect

Flexible, transparent conductive traces routed to the temple driver board.

07
Frame channel

Precision-milled titanium or acetate channel that seats and seals the lens stack.

14 · Smart features

What the AI layer actually does

Environment recognition

Fuses light, motion, and phone context into a confidence score per mode, rather than a single hard rule.

Tint prediction

Anticipates upcoming light changes (e.g. stepping outside a building) from motion trajectory, smoothing the transition.

Comfort recommendations

Surfaces patterns like "you dim the tint every day around 4pm" as a suggested profile tweak — never applied without a tap.

Usage & strain reminders

Flags long unbroken close-focus sessions with a gentle break reminder, based on published visual-ergonomics guidance.

Mode prediction

Learns your routine (commute, desk hours) to pre-load the likely next mode a few seconds early.

Prescription management

Tracks profile version history and flags when a prescription is nearing its typical 1–2 year revalidation window.

NOTE These are personalization and convenience features, not medical guidance. EYE ON YOU does not diagnose eye disease, determine a prescription independently, or replace a comprehensive eye exam.
