# EYE ON YOU — Supplier Outreach Message Template

Use this for the first message to each supplier on Alibaba / Global Sources / Made-in-China. Keep the first message short — the full spec goes out only after they respond and (ideally) sign the NNN.

---

## First contact message (short version — send this first)

> Hello,
>
> I'm developing a premium smart eyewear product (adaptive tint lens, Bluetooth, ambient light sensor, companion app) and I'm looking for a manufacturing partner for an initial sample run.
>
> Before I share detailed specs, could you tell me:
> 1. Have you previously manufactured smart glasses, electrochromic/photochromic lenses, or similar wearable electronics? Any reference products or case studies?
> 2. Are you open to signing an NDA/NNN agreement before receiving detailed design files?
> 3. What is your typical sample lead time and MOQ for a product like this?
>
> If this is a good fit, I'll follow up with a full technical specification and NDA.
>
> Thank you,
> [Your name]
> [Your company name, if any]
> [Email]

---

## Follow-up message (after they respond positively)

> Thanks for getting back to me. Attached are:
> 1. An NNN (Non-Disclosure, Non-Use, Non-Circumvention) agreement — please review and sign before I send the full spec.
> 2. Once signed, I'll send the technical specification and RFQ with the exact components, quantities, and quote request.
>
> A few quick questions in the meantime:
> - What certifications do you currently hold (ISO 9001, CE testing capability, RoHS compliance)?
> - Can you handle UN38.3 battery certification for air freight?
> - What's your typical payment structure for sample orders (e.g., 100% upfront, 50/50)?
>
> Looking forward to working together.

---

## Notes

- **Send to 10–20 suppliers minimum.** Expect maybe 2–5 serious responses, and 1–2 worth actually sampling from.
- **Never send the full spec/RFQ on first contact.** It's your IP — the short version above filters out suppliers who won't sign an NNN or don't have relevant experience, before you show them anything real.
- **Red flags to walk away from:** anyone who says they can deliver a working sample in under 2 weeks for this product, anyone unwilling to discuss certifications, anyone asking for full payment before any sample exists.
- **Green flags:** they ask clarifying technical questions back, they can name specific past wearable/eyewear electronics projects, they're comfortable with a staged payment structure (deposit + balance on inspection).
