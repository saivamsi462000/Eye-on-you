The problem

Most people carry four pairs of glasses through one day

Prescription glasses

For distance vision — driving, meetings, walking around.

Reading glasses

Swapped in for books, menus, small print.

Computer glasses

A different focal length for the screen in front of you.

Sunglasses

Left in the car, the bag, or the wrong room, every time.

What's inside

Built around four ideas

Adaptive prescription

Electronically adjustable optics reproduce your verified prescription, set by your eye-care professional — never inferred from a camera.

Eye Comfort Mode

Adaptive brightness, blue-light management, and anti-glare filtering reduce contributors to visual strain through the day.

Smart sunglasses

One electrochromic lens shifts continuously from fully clear to a deep sunglasses tint as light changes.

EYE ON YOU app

A minimal companion app for modes, prescription profiles, battery, and firmware — full manual control, always available.
